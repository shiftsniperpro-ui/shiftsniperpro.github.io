Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Information We Collect

Shift Sniper Pro collects minimal information necessary to provide the service:

  • Email address (used to identify your account and subscription)
  • Extension version number (for compatibility purposes)
  • Credit usage data (to track your daily scan usage)

We do NOT collect, store, or transmit your Amazon login credentials. Your username and PIN are stored locally on your device only using Chrome's local storage.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To verify your subscription status and credit balance
  • To send your license key and welcome email after payment
  • To provide customer support
  • To improve the extension

3. Data Storage

Your account data (email, plan, credits) is stored securely in our database. Your Amazon credentials are stored locally on your device and never leave your browser.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Buy Me a Coffee — payment processing and subscription management
  • Google Sheets / Apps Script — backend database and API
  • Gmail — sending license keys and notifications

5. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as you have an active subscription. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

6. Your Rights

  • Right to access your personal data
  • Right to correct inaccurate data
  • Right to delete your data
  • Right to cancel your subscription at any time

7. Contact

For any privacy-related questions, contact us at: support.shiftsniperpro@gmail.com
