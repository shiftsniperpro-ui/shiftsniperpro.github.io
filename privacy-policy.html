Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Shift Sniper Pro ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our Chrome extension. By using Shift Sniper Pro, you agree to this policy.

1. Who We Are

Shift Sniper Pro is a Chrome browser extension developed and operated by an independent developer based in Ontario, Canada. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Amazon.com Inc. in any way.

Contact: support.shiftsniperpro@gmail.com

2. Information We Collect

When you use Shift Sniper Pro, we collect the following information:

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Full name (first and last)
  • Email address (your Amazon account email)
  • Phone number
  • Date of birth
  • Home address (street, city, province, postal code)
  • Work availability preferences (shift times, available days)
  • Employment history (optional)
  • Education history (optional)
  • License key (your unique subscription key)

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • IP address (to detect unauthorized sharing of license keys)
  • Device type (Mac, Windows, Android)
  • Browser version
  • Geographic location (city and province, derived from IP)
  • Number of scans performed
  • Number of jobs found and booked
  • Date and time of extension usage
  • Last active timestamp
  • Amazon account email (to detect multiple account usage)

2.3 Job Market Data

  • When a job is detected, we record: city, province, time, job type, and shift type
  • This data is anonymized and used to build job availability patterns
  • No personal information is attached to job market reports

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the service: Your personal information is used to auto-fill Amazon job application forms on your behalf
  • License verification: Your license key is verified against our database to confirm your subscription is active
  • Anti-fraud protection: IP address and device data is used to prevent unauthorized sharing of license keys
  • Usage tracking: Scan counts and activity data help us improve the service and enforce plan limits
  • Job market intelligence: Anonymized job detection data is aggregated to identify patterns in Amazon hiring
  • Customer support: Your contact information may be used to respond to support requests
  • Subscription management: To manage your subscription status, renewals, and cancellations

4. Data Storage

Your personal profile data (name, address, phone etc.) is stored locally on your device only using Chrome's secure storage API. This data never leaves your device except when used to fill Amazon's application forms directly.

Usage data (scans, activity, license status) is stored in a secure Google Sheets database accessible only to the developer.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties except:

  • Amazon.ca: Your profile data is submitted directly to Amazon's hiring forms when you authorize the extension to apply on your behalf
  • Google: Usage data is stored in Google Sheets (Google's privacy policy applies)
  • Gumroad: Payment processing (Gumroad's privacy policy applies)
  • Legal requirements: If required by Canadian law

6. Data Retention

  • Personal profile data: Stored locally until you clear it or uninstall the extension
  • License and usage data: Retained for 2 years after subscription ends
  • Job market data: Retained indefinitely in anonymized form

7. Your Rights (PIPEDA — Canadian Privacy Law)

Under Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time (subject to contractual obligations)
  • File a complaint with the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada

To exercise these rights, contact us at: support.shiftsniperpro@gmail.com

8. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information including:

  • All data transmission uses HTTPS encryption
  • Personal profile data stored locally on your device only
  • License database access restricted to developer only
  • No passwords or financial information stored by our extension

9. Children's Privacy

Shift Sniper Pro is intended for users 18 years of age and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you are under 18, do not use this extension.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify active subscribers by email when significant changes are made. Continued use of the extension after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
