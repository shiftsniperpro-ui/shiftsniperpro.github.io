FAQ

Common questions.

Everything you need to know before getting started.

How quickly does the extension find shifts?
The extension scans Amazon's job listings every few seconds. VIP users get the fastest scan speed at 0.5 seconds. When a matching shift is found it automatically applies within milliseconds.
Is my Amazon account safe?
Yes. Your Amazon login credentials are stored locally on your device only — never on our servers. The extension logs in through Amazon's official website just like you would manually.
What cities are supported?
Basic covers 26 Canadian cities. Pro and VIP cover 69 cities across Canada and the USA including Toronto, Brampton, Vancouver, Calgary, Edmonton, Mississauga, and many more.
What happens when my credits run out?
Credits reset daily based on your plan. If you somehow run out before the reset, the extension will pause and show you upgrade options. Credits never expire — unused ones carry over.
How do I get my license key after paying?
Your license key is emailed to you within 1 hour of payment at the email you used on Buy Me a Coffee. Contact support.shiftsniperpro@gmail.com if you don't receive it.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes — you can cancel your membership anytime through Buy Me a Coffee. Note there is a 6 month minimum contract period. Contact us if you have questions about your subscription.
