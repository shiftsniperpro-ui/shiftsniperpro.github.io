SECTION 01

About Shift Sniper Pro

Shift Sniper Pro is a Google Chrome browser extension designed to automate the process of monitoring Amazon's warehouse hiring pages across Canada and booking available shifts on your behalf the moment they appear. The extension runs continuously in the background of your browser, scanning Amazon's hiring platform at regular intervals depending on your subscription plan, and when a shift becomes available it immediately begins the application process using your saved personal information.

It is critically important to understand that Shift Sniper Pro is a completely independent product. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or connected in any way to Amazon.com Inc., Amazon Canada Fulfillment Services, or any of their subsidiaries or partners. The use of the word "Amazon" in our description refers solely to Amazon's publicly accessible hiring platform and does not imply any official relationship or partnership whatsoever.

Shift Sniper Pro is intended exclusively for individuals who are 18 years of age or older and who are legally eligible to work in Canada. By using this service you represent and confirm that you meet both of these requirements.

SECTION 05

Scan Limits Per Plan

Each subscription plan comes with a defined maximum number of scans the extension is permitted to perform per calendar day. The Basic plan allows a maximum of ten thousand scans per day. The Pro plan allows a maximum of twenty-five thousand scans per day. The VIP plan has no daily scan limit and runs without any restriction. All scan limits reset at midnight Eastern Standard Time every calendar day. If you reach your daily scan limit the extension automatically pauses until the reset occurs at midnight. Reaching your daily scan limit does not entitle you to a refund, a free plan upgrade, or any form of compensation.

SECTION 07

Privacy & Data We Collect

Shift Sniper Pro collects personal information from you in two ways — information you provide directly and information collected automatically. We are committed to handling your information responsibly and in compliance with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

The personal information you provide directly includes your first and last name, your email address which must match your Amazon account, your phone number, your date of birth, your complete home address including street, city, province, and postal code, your work availability preferences including preferred shifts and days of the week, and optionally your employment history and education history to improve the accuracy of auto-filled application forms.

We also collect certain information automatically when you use the extension. This includes your IP address which we use to detect unauthorized license sharing, your device type and operating system, your browser version, the number of scans you perform each day, the number of jobs detected and booked on your behalf, the timestamps of your usage, and your Amazon account email specifically to detect if a single license key is being used with multiple Amazon accounts.

When the extension detects an available job posting, it sends a brief anonymized report to our database recording the city, province, time of day, job type, and shift type of that posting. This report contains none of your personal details and is used solely to build a picture of when and where Amazon posts warehouse jobs across Canada.

SECTION 08

How We Use Your Data

Your personal profile information — your name, address, phone number, date of birth, and employment details — is stored locally on your own device using Chrome's secure built-in storage. This information never leaves your device except when you authorize the extension to submit a job application on Amazon's platform on your behalf, at which point it is entered directly into Amazon's forms exactly as you would type it yourself.

Your license key and usage data including scan counts, job detection records, last active time, IP address, and device type are stored in a secure database accessible only to the developer. This data is used to verify your subscription, enforce your daily scan limits, detect unauthorized license sharing, track job market patterns across Canada, and provide customer support. We do not sell your personal information to any third party under any circumstances and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

SECTION 09

Data Sharing & Security

The only third parties with whom your information may be shared are Amazon.ca when your application is submitted on your behalf, Google in connection with the Google Sheets infrastructure that powers our license management system, and Gumroad or another payment processor in connection with processing your subscription payments. We may also disclose information where required by Canadian law. All communication between the extension and our license server takes place over encrypted HTTPS connections. Your personal profile data never travels to our servers and remains entirely on your own device. We retain your license and usage data for two years following the end of your subscription. Anonymized job market data is retained indefinitely.
